
# Daily Stand-up Meeting Script

## Characters

-   **Suraj** -- Team Lead (You)
-   **Rahul** -- Operations Executive
-   **Anita** -- Coordinator
-   **Vikram** -- Support Executive
-   **Emma** -- English Coach

## Opening

### Suraj

Good morning, everyone.

Thank you for joining today's stand-up meeting.

This meeting will take about fifteen minutes.

Today's agenda: - Yesterday's work - Today's priorities - Blockers -
Support needed

Can everyone hear me clearly?

### Rahul

Yes, loud and clear.

### Anita

Good morning. Ready to start.

### Vikram

Yes. Let's begin.

## Yesterday's Work

### Suraj

Rahul, could you start with yesterday's update?

### Rahul

I completed the shipment report. I spoke with two vendors. I couldn't
finish the inventory check. I'll complete it today.

### Suraj

Thank you. Good progress.

## Today's Plan

### Suraj

Anita, what are your priorities today?

### Anita

I have to prepare the weekly report. I may also help Rahul with
documentation.

### Suraj

Great. Please update the tracker before lunch.

## Blockers

### Suraj

Vikram, do you have any blockers?

### Vikram

Yes. The printer isn't working. We might have to replace it if repairs
fail.

### Suraj

Please keep us updated.

## Support

### Suraj

Does anyone need help today?

### Rahul

Could someone review my report?

### Anita

Yes, I can help.

### Suraj

Thank you.

## Closing

### Suraj

Today's action items: - Rahul will finish the inventory check. - Anita
will prepare the weekly report. - Vikram will resolve the printer
issue. - Everyone should update the tracker before the end of the day.

Thank you, everyone. Have a productive day!

## Emma's Grammar Tips

-   ✅ I can send it.
-   ✅ We should wait.
-   ✅ He doesn't know.

Priority Productive Inventory Documentation Update Progress Schedule
Completed Support Responsibility


# Interview Practice Script

## Characters

-   **Interviewer (Mr. James)**
-   **Suraj (Candidate -- You)**
-   **Emma (English Coach)**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Introduction

### Interviewer

Good morning.

Please introduce yourself.

### Suraj

Good morning.

Thank you for giving me this opportunity.

My name is Suraj.

I work in operations and linehaul management.

I monitor vendor performance, coordinate with different teams, and help
ensure on-time operations.

### Emma

Tip: - Speak slowly. - Smile. - Pause after each sentence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Question 1 -- Tell me about yourself.

### Interviewer

Can you tell me about yourself?

### Suraj

Certainly.

I have experience in logistics and operations.

My responsibilities include tracking shipments, monitoring vendor
performance, resolving operational issues, and improving daily
processes.

I enjoy solving problems and working with teams.

### Emma

Natural phrase: "I enjoy working in a fast-paced environment."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Question 2 -- Why do you want this job?

### Interviewer

Why do you want to join our company?

### Suraj

I believe this role matches my skills and experience.

I want to grow professionally, learn new processes, and contribute to
the company's success.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Question 3 -- Strengths

### Interviewer

What are your strengths?

### Suraj

I communicate well with teams.

I stay calm under pressure.

I can solve problems quickly.

I always try to improve processes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Question 4 -- Weakness

### Interviewer

What is one area you are improving?

### Suraj

Sometimes I spend too much time checking details.

I am learning to balance accuracy with speed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Question 5 -- Difficult Situation

### Interviewer

Tell me about a challenging situation.

### Suraj

A vendor repeatedly delayed shipments.

I discussed the issue with the vendor, tracked the performance, and
escalated the problem when necessary.

The delays were reduced after we introduced regular reviews.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Question 6 -- Teamwork

### Interviewer

How do you work with your team?

### Suraj

I listen carefully.

I communicate clearly.

I support my teammates whenever needed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Question 7 -- Future Goals

### Interviewer

Where do you see yourself in five years?

### Suraj

I hope to grow into a leadership role.

I want to manage larger projects and continue improving my communication
and management skills.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Closing

### Interviewer

Do you have any questions for us?

### Suraj

Yes.

Could you tell me about the training process?

What does success look like in this role?

Thank you for your time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Emma's Grammar Tips

❌ I am working since 2022. ✅ I have been working since 2022.

❌ He don't know. ✅ He doesn't know.

❌ I can to do this. ✅ I can do this.
